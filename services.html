Services

Website design services

Vikas Digital builds websites for small businesses through fixed-price packages. The core services below are included in one or more packages; the add-on services can be added to any package at the prices shown. Your final scope is always confirmed in a written proposal before work begins.

Business Website Design

A professionally designed website for your business, built to look credible on phones, tablets and desktops. Page counts depend on your package: up to 5 pages with Starter, up to 10 pages with Professional, and up to 15 pages with Elite. Additional pages can be added to any package at $100 per page.

Included in: Starter, Professional, Elite

CMS & Blog Setup

Your website is set up on a content management system so you can update pages and publish blog posts yourself, without paying for every small change. CMS and blog setup are included in the Professional and Elite packages.

Included in: Professional, Elite

Analytics Setup

Analytics is set up on your website so you can see how many people visit, where they come from and which pages they use. Included in the Professional and Elite packages. Elite adds conversion tracking, so enquiries and other key actions can be measured as well.

Included in: Professional (analytics setup), Elite (analytics setup + conversion tracking)

Online Booking

Let customers book appointments or services directly through your website. Advanced booking is included in the Elite package, and booking can be added to Starter or Professional as an add-on from $300.

Included in: Elite (advanced booking); add-on to other packages from $300

E-commerce

Sell products through your website with an online shop added to your design. E-commerce is available as an add-on to any package, from $750. The exact scope — number of products, payment and delivery setup — is confirmed in your written proposal.

Available as: Add-on, from $750

Logo Design

A logo designed for your business, supplied in the formats you need for your website and everyday use. Available as an add-on to any package.

Available as: Add-on, $150

Website Copywriting

Don't want to write your own website text? Copywriting is available as an add-on: the words for your pages are written for you, based on information you provide about your business.

Available as: Add-on, $200

Website Maintenance

After launch, optional maintenance keeps your website looked after month to month. Maintenance is a rolling monthly add-on and can be started or stopped as your needs change. What is covered in a given month is confirmed in writing when you sign up.

Available as: Add-on, $99/month

Not sure which services you need?

Tell us about your business and we'll recommend the package and add-ons that fit — confirmed in a written proposal before any work begins.
