Refund Policy

Draft — pending final review.

1. How payments work

Vikas Digital does not take payments through this website — there is no checkout on this site. Every engagement is agreed in a written proposal before work begins, and that proposal sets out the amounts payable, the payment methods and the payment schedule for the project (for example, whether payment is made in stages, and what each stage covers). Where anything in this policy differs from your written proposal, the written proposal prevails.

2. General principles

Work not started. If an engagement is cancelled before work on a stage described in the proposal has begun, amounts paid for that unstarted stage are treated as set out in the written proposal.

Work in progress or completed. Amounts paid for work that has been carried out are not refundable, because that work cannot be undone. What counts as carried out is judged against the stages and deliverables described in the written proposal.

Deposits. Where a proposal includes a deposit or booking payment, whether it is refundable, and in what circumstances, is as set out in the written proposal.

3. Add-ons

One-off add-ons (such as logo design, copywriting or extra pages) follow the same principle: they are agreed in writing before the work is done, and the treatment of any payment if the add-on is cancelled is as agreed at that time. The maintenance add-on is billed monthly; how cancellation of maintenance mid-month is treated is confirmed in writing when you sign up.

4. If something goes wrong

If you believe work delivered does not match what your written proposal describes, tell us through the contact form. Our first step is to put the work right within the scope of the proposal — including the revision rounds your package includes — rather than treat the matter as a refund question.

5. How to request a refund

Send your request through the contact form on this website, with your name, the proposal the payment relates to, and the reason for the request. Requests are assessed against this policy and the written proposal.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Engagements already agreed under a written proposal are governed by that proposal's terms.
