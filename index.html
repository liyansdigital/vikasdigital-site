FAQ

Questions, answered

The details people usually ask about first — pricing, timelines, revisions and what happens after launch.

Our websites are sold as fixed-price packages: Starter is $749 for up to 5 pages, Professional is $1,499 for up to 10 pages with CMS/blog and analytics setup, and Elite is $2,999 for up to 15 pages with advanced booking and conversion tracking. Add-ons are priced individually — for example extra pages at $100 each and copywriting at $200. Your final price is confirmed in a written proposal before any work begins.

Timelines depend on the package: Starter takes 7–10 business days, Professional 12–18 business days, and Elite 20–30 business days. The timeline starts once your proposal is approved and we have the content we need from you.

Starter includes 2 revision rounds, Professional includes 3, and Elite includes 5. Revisions happen at the review stage, after the site is built. If you need more, extra revision rounds are available at $50 each.
