Privacy Policy

Draft — pending final review.

1. Who we are

Vikas Digital is a website design studio founded by Vikas Kumar Meena, based in Surajgarh, Rajasthan, India. This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why, and what your choices are. Privacy questions and requests can be sent through the contact form on this website.

2. Information we collect

Enquiry form. When you send an enquiry through our contact form, we collect the information you enter: your name, email address, business name, current website (if provided) and the contents of your message. We need this to reply to you and to prepare any proposal you ask for.

Project information. If you become a client, we will hold the materials and details you provide for your project (such as content for your website and contact details for day-to-day communication).

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting and security systems automatically record technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, in server logs used to operate and protect the site.

Cookies. This site uses cookies as described in our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use your information

We use your information:

• To respond to enquiries and provide proposals
• To deliver and support the services you engage us for
• To operate, secure and improve this website
• To meet legal or accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use enquiry information for unrelated marketing, and we do not send newsletters to people who have not asked for them.

4. Who we share information with

We share personal information only where needed to run the website and deliver services — for example with our website hosting provider and the tools used to receive form submissions — and where required by law. These providers process information on our instructions and for those purposes only.

5. How long we keep information

Enquiry information is kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any follow-up, and client project information for as long as needed to deliver and support the engagement and meet record-keeping obligations. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us: what personal information we hold about you; to correct it; to delete it; or to stop using it for a particular purpose. Send any such request through the contact form and we will respond as required by law.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including access controls on the systems that store it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Links to other websites

This website may link to other sites (for example, client sites in a portfolio). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of sites we do not operate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is the one published on this page, with its "last updated" date.

10. Contact

For anything in this policy, contact us through the contact form on this website.
