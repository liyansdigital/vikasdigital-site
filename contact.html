Contact Vikas Digital

Tell us about your business and the website you need, using the form below. Every enquiry is read, and the next step is a discovery conversation followed by a written proposal with a fixed price and timeline — no obligation, and no work begins until you've approved that proposal.

What to include in your enquiry

The more of this you can share, the more useful the proposal will be:

  • Your business name and what you do
  • Whether you need a new website or a replacement for an existing one (include your current web address if you have one)
  • The package you're interested in — Starter ($749), Professional ($1,499) or Elite ($2,999) — or "not sure yet"
  • Any add-ons you're considering: extra pages, logo design, copywriting, e-commerce, booking, maintenance
  • Anything else the website needs to do

Already have a website? Ask for a free review

If your business already has a website, use the same form and mention you'd like a free review. We'll look at how the site presents your business and what could be improved, and include our findings in the reply.
