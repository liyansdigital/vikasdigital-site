About Vikas Digital

Vikas Digital is a website design studio founded by Vikas Kumar Meena and based in Surajgarh, Rajasthan, India. The studio designs and builds websites for small businesses that need a professional online presence without agency complexity or open-ended costs.

What we do

We build websites through three fixed-price packages — Starter, Professional and Elite — covering everything from a simple five-page site to a fifteen-page site with online booking and conversion tracking. Optional add-ons cover logo design, copywriting, e-commerce and ongoing maintenance, so a project can stay small or grow as the business does.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a conversation about your business, followed by a written proposal that confirms the scope, the fixed price and the timeline before any work begins. You review the site at a dedicated review stage, using the revision rounds included in your package, and the site launches only when the agreed work is complete.

What we don't do

We don't sell rankings we can't guarantee, and we don't pad projects with services you didn't ask for. If something isn't in your written proposal, it isn't in your project — and it isn't on your bill.
