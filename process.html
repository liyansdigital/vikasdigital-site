Process

How we work

Every Vikas Digital project runs through the same seven steps. The process is designed so you always know what has been done, what is happening now, and what you need to do next.

  1. Discovery

    We start with a conversation about your business: what you offer, who your customers are, and what your website needs to do for you. This is also where we agree which package fits — Starter, Professional or Elite — and whether you need any add-ons such as a logo, copywriting, booking or e-commerce.

  2. Proposal

    You receive a written proposal confirming the package, the exact scope, the fixed price and the timeline. Nothing is built and nothing is charged until you have approved that proposal. If it isn't in the proposal, it isn't in the project.

  3. Design

    With the proposal approved, your website's design is created: the layout, look and structure of your pages, based on the content and materials gathered in discovery. Professional and Elite projects also have their CMS/blog and analytics setup planned at this stage.

  4. Build

    The approved design is built into a working website — pages constructed, features added, and (by package) the CMS, blog, analytics, booking or e-commerce set up and connected.

  5. Review

    You review the built website and send your changes. Every package includes revision rounds at this stage:

    • Starter: 2 revision rounds
    • Professional: 3 revision rounds
    • Elite: 5 revision rounds

    Need more rounds? Extra revision rounds are available at $50 each.

  6. Launch

    Once the review changes are complete and the site matches the agreed scope, your website goes live. Timelines by package, counted in business days from proposal approval and receipt of the content we need from you:

    • Starter: 7–10 business days
    • Professional: 12–18 business days
    • Elite: 20–30 business days

  7. Maintenance (optional)

    After launch, you can manage the site yourself — Professional and Elite sites include a CMS for exactly that — or hand the ongoing care to us with the maintenance add-on at $99/month. Maintenance is optional and rolling; what is covered is confirmed in writing when you sign up.

Ready when you are.
