1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember choices you make, and — where used — help the site owner understand how the site is being visited. This policy explains the categories of cookies used on this website and how you can control them.

2. Essential cookies

These cookies are needed for the website to function and stay secure — for example, cookies that support page navigation, form submission and protection against malicious traffic. The website cannot work properly without them, so they do not require consent.

3. Functional cookies

These cookies remember choices you make on the site (such as display preferences) so the site behaves the way you set it.

4. Analytics cookies

Where analytics is enabled on this website, analytics cookies help us count visits and understand which pages are used, so we can improve the site. Note: "analytics setup" sold as a client service is separate from analytics on this website.

5. Third-party cookies

Some pages may include content or features from third parties (for example, embedded media), which can set their own cookies under their own policies. We do not control those cookies.

6. How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of this website — including the contact form — from working correctly. Most browsers also offer a "do not track" or private-browsing mode; consult your browser's help pages for instructions.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the cookies in use change. The current version is the one published on this page, with its "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about cookies can be sent through the contact form on this website.
